VrFsim is a PC VR practice simulator for the *FIRST* Tech Challenge 2026–27 game, **BIOBUZZ**. Put on your headset, stand at the driver station like a real DRIVER, and drive a simulated FTC robot on a full-size field with an ordinary gamepad.

This is the **recommended** version: the game runs on your PC and you play in the headset through Quest Link, Air Link, SteamVR or any OpenXR runtime. The window on your monitor shows what the player sees, so others can watch. No headset? It also runs on the monitor.

## Features
- Full 144 in BIOBUZZ field: two tipping HIVES, four FLOWERS, LOADING ZONES, GARDENS and all 56 scoring elements, staged per the Competition Manual
- Real match flow: 0:30 AUTO, 0:08 transition, 2:00 TELEOP, field audio cues, scoring with ranking points and an end-of-match results screen
- Build your robot like in dsim: mecanum, tank, swerve, X-drive or butterfly drivetrain, intakes (single, double, side), turret, double turret, dumper, Box Tube, team name and colours
- Physically simulated driving with motor and traction physics from goBILDA hardware
- Every setting in an in-VR menu, saved between sessions

## Controls
- Any controller works: Xbox, PlayStation, Logitech (X or D mode), Switch Pro and other USB pads, the VR controllers, or the keyboard
- Left stick: drive and strafe. Right stick: turn
- `LT` intake, `RT` fire, `A` start the match, `Start` menu, `D-pad Right` next camera view, `D-pad Left` recenter
- Every button can be rebound in Menu → Controls

## System requirements
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- A VR-ready GPU (GTX 1070 / RX 5700 class or better)
- Any PC VR headset with an OpenXR runtime (Meta Quest through Link, Valve Index, HTC Vive, WMR…)

Laptop too weak for Quest Link? Install **VrFsim (Meta Quest)** on the headset instead.
